Summary
▪ Most software systems today are insecure and contain numerous flaws and bugs
that attackers exploit.
▪ Defects, Bugs, Flaws, Risks, and vulnerabilities are the terms used in software.
▪ There are three pillars in software security: risk management, touchpoints, and
knowledge.
▪ Threat modeling is a structured process used in cybersecurity to identify, assess,
and mitigate security risks within a system, application, or network.
