Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) (II)
1.Planning: Identifying project goals, requirements, and feasibility.
2.Design: Creating architecture and design specifications.
3.Implementation: Writing and compiling the code.
4.Testing: Validating the software against requirements and identifying
defects.
5.Deployment: Releasing the software to users.
6.Maintenance: Ongoing support and updates post-deployment.
