Terminology (I)
• Defects are problems that deviates from the requirements and specification.
o Example: The requirement says the app must allow password of at least 8 characters, but the
app allows 5 characters.
• Bugs are implementation-level or coding errors that cause software to behave
incorrectly
o Example: The app crashes when click “Login” button.
• Flaws are weakness or imperfection in the design, architecture, or logic that may
lead to problems.
o Example: The app stores passwords in plain text instead of encrypted. This is a design flaw.
• Failures are the inability of the software to perform its required function.
