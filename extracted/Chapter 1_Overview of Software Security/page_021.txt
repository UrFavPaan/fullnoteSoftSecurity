1- Abuse Cases (I)
• Determining what the software can't and won't do (No positive
features). Example: users can't enter more than 50 characters.
• Must anticipate abnormal behaviors.
• Don't accept the idea that says, "No one would do these things".
• What happens when functional security mechanisms are
compromised?
