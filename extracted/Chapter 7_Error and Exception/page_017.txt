Best Practices in Secure Exception Handling
Catch specific
exceptions, not
general ones.
Never expose
stack traces or
system internals to
end-users.
Always log
exceptions
securely (for
admins, not users).
Use finally or try-
with-resources to
release resources.
Design custom
exception classes
for clarity.
