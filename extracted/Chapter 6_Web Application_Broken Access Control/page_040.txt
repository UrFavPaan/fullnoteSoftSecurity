Real-World Scenarios: Broken Function
Level Authorization (BFLA)
• Complex access control policies with different
hierarchies, groups, and roles, and an unclear
separation between administrative and regular
functions, tend to lead to authorization flaws.
• By exploiting these issues, attackers gain access to
other users’ resources and/or administrative
functions.
