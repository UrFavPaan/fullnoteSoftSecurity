Current Frameworks and Models
• There are several static analysis frameworks
available, such as SonarQube, Coverity, and
Checkmarx.
• These frameworks use various models to analyze
code, including control flow models, data flow
models, and abstract syntax trees.
• Each framework has its own strengths and
weaknesses, and the selection of a framework
depends on the specific requirements of the
project.
