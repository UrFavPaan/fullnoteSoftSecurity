Factors of static analysis framework
• When selecting a static analysis framework, it is
important to consider several factors. Cost is one
consideration, as some frameworks may be more
expensive than others.
• Ease of use is also important, as some frameworks
may require specialized expertise to use effectively.
Integration with other tools, such as development
environments or bug tracking systems, can also be
important for streamlining the development
process.
