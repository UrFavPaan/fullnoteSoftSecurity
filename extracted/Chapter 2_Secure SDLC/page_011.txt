1. Planning & Requirements Phase
• Risk Assessment: Conduct a thorough risk assessment to identify
potential security threats and vulnerabilities.
• Abuse Cases: Provide insights into how attackers will approach the
system's assumptions.
• Security Requirements: Define security and functional requirements
to ensure they are considered immediately.
