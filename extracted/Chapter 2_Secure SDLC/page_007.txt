Software Security Maturity Model (V)
• Step 1: Assessment from
OWASP SAMM
They had strong compliance
policies (Governance: Level 2)
but weak secure coding practices
(Implementation: Level 1).
• Step 2: Set a Target Maturity
Level
Business decides they need Level
2 maturity in all domains within
2 years to reduce risks.
• Step 3: Improvement Plan
• Governance: Establish a formal AppSec steering
committee.
• Design: Adopt threat modeling workshops for
high-risk applications.
• Implementation: Train developers on OWASP Top
10, introduce SAST/DAST tools.
• Verification: Add regular penetration tests and
CI/CD security checks.
• Operations: Develop incident response playbooks
and security monitoring dashboards.
